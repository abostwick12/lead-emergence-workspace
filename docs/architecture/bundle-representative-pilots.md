## Product experience

The value-check page now contains a collapsed “Use a comparable pilot” guide for
each bundle. Opening it reveals a fictional rehearsal notice, participant fit,
pre-timer setup, a baseline question, a source packet, timed tasks with observable
completion evidence, and a bundle-specific quality bar. The measurement controls
remain outside the guide and work exactly as they did in P16.

The six scenarios exercise the promises that matter most:

- Executive prioritizes permitted task metadata without exposing domain bodies.
- Writer & Editor combines voice, revision, metadata, relationship, link, and
  publishing checks without a provider mutation.
- Ministry preserves source layers, fictional citation status, prior-work status,
  and client-owned belief boundaries.
- Nonprofit Founder sequences administrative work, preserves source-first
  regulatory uncertainty, and blocks clinical or unauthorized outreach behavior.
- Investor separates facts, interpretation, thesis, scenarios, and predictions,
  seeks counterevidence, and performs no trade.
- Workspace Experience composes entitlements, prioritizes attention, navigates to
  exact sources, and requires confirmation for persistent layout changes.

## Portable evidence contract

The source package owns the six version-pinned definitions and a structured run
record. Run records admit scenario/bundle IDs, a value-pilot UUID, rehearsal or
authorized-representative mode, timestamp, facilitator-action count, bounded issue
codes, and the already-bounded P16 timing/outcome/rating/gate fields. They admit no
participant name, prompt, source, output, notes, provider content, or client work.

The cohort evaluator excludes every `synthetic_rehearsal` attempt. A controlled-
beta advance requires at least three authorized representative attempts, at least
two unaided attempts, two-thirds completion/outcome/target rates, median ratings
of at least 4/5, median corrections no greater than two, all trust gates, and no
critical safety issue. Abandonment remains evidence rather than disappearing.

`advance_to_controlled_beta` applies only to the bounded scenario. It is not a
production-readiness, installed-host, provider, commercial, causality, or general
indispensability claim.

## Authority and fixture boundary

The checked-in packets are static fictional instructions. The UI never seeds them
into a user's workspace, never treats them as an authority source, and never
stores them in value-measurement rows. A pilot operator may prepare them manually
in an isolated rehearsal workspace or substitute explicitly authorized,
participant-owned material that fits the same task.

No database schema or P16 authorization path changes in this milestone. The host
renders the pinned definitions as a guide; the portable evaluator is the release-
decision oracle. A later authorized representative run must still use the native
private P16 measurement flow and retain all attempted runs, including failures.
